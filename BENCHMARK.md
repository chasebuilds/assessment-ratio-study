# Benchmark

Scored on 30 seeded synthetic counties (seeds 1 to 30), about 840 sales each. Generated by `benchmark/run_benchmark.py`; the numbers below come from that run.

## Planted problems (one per neighborhood, 8 residential neighborhoods per county)

| Planted problem | Where | Detected by | Caught |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Regressivity (ratio falls about 12% per doubling of price) | Maple Hills | PRB marginal or fail | 30/30 |
| Same, strict | Maple Hills | PRB beyond +/-0.10 | 24/30 |
| Under-assessment (median ratio 0.80) | Old Town | median outside 0.90-1.10 | 30/30 |
| High dispersion (ratio spread 0.24 log sd) | Riverbend | COD above 15 | 0/30 |
| Same, counting the trim-rate warning | Riverbend | COD above 15, or over 10% of sales trimmed | 22/30 |
| Sales chasing (only sold parcels reset to price) | Ridgeview | sold vs unsold flag | 30/30 |

**Finding: the Riverbend COD check misses every time.** The IQR trim runs per property class and cuts the wide tails of a high-dispersion neighborhood, so its COD after trimming lands under the limit. The report catches it only through the trim-rate warning (over 10% of the neighborhood's sales trimmed), which fires in the second row. A reviewer who looks only at COD would be fooled; read the trim rate next to it.

Ridgeview's ratio table looks excellent (median about 1.00, COD about 2), so the ratio statistics alone miss it. Ratio-table verdict was PASS in 30/30 counties.

## False alarms on clean strata

Six clean strata per county (4 neighborhoods, commercial, vacant land), 180 stratum-runs in all.

- FAIL on a clean stratum: 1 (0.6%)
- REVIEW on a clean stratum: 5 (2.8%)
- Sales-chasing flag on a clean neighborhood: 0

## Rising market (12% a year, static roll)

Same 30 seeds, but sale prices drift up 12% over the year while assessments stay at their January values. Time adjustment (`--time-adjust auto`) estimates the trend and restates prices at the earliest sale date.

| | Regressivity caught | Under-assessment caught | Sales chasing caught | FAIL or REVIEW on clean strata | Mean COD, clean neighborhoods |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| No adjustment | 30/30 | 30/30 | 30/30 | 22.2% | 6.52 |
| Auto time adjustment | 30/30 | 30/30 | 30/30 | 2.8% | 5.92 |

Estimated trend across seeds: mean 12.3% a year (range 9.9% to 14.3%) against 12.0% planted.

## What this does not show

- The data is synthetic and the planted effects are large on purpose. Smaller effects will be missed more often; a real roll has problems that are not planted here.
- Detection depends on the stratum having at least 30 sales.
- Results here say how the checks behave on this generator, not on any real county.
